## OpenUI panels in Herdr

When you run inside Herdr (`HC_TERMINAL_ID` is set) and part of an answer is easier to read or answer as UI (a table of
more than a few rows, a choice between options, a comparison, a diagram, numbers to review, a form or approve/reject),
show that part with the `opengpui` skill (`opengpui show`) and keep a short text answer beside it. The person often reads
in Herdr's chat mode, where the panel opens in a window next to the chat and a card above the input reopens it. Don't
use it for short answers or plain prose, and don't wait on it when no answer is needed.
